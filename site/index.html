Retro Games Mobile

Privacy policy

Effective August 8, 2026

Summary

Retro Games Mobile is provided by The Funky Bits. The Android application dev.thefunkybits.rgm does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, cloud service, or application-operated server.

Files you select

You choose a folder containing software files that you lawfully possess. Android grants the app read access to that selected location. RGM reads those files locally to identify a supported release and creates a private installation in the app's local storage. The selected and installed files are not uploaded by RGM.

The Android folder-access grant may remain until you revoke it in system settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Local data, retention, and deletion

Installed content, emulator configuration, local save archives, settings, and play history remain on your device. RGM retains each item until you use an applicable in-app removal action, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Clearing data or uninstalling the app removes its app-private data.

RGM excludes its app-private data from Android backup and device transfer. This release does not synchronize local data to a cloud service.

Network access and security

The app does not declare Android's INTERNET permission and does not upload selected files, installations, saves, settings, or play history. App-private files are protected by Android's application storage controls.

Notifications

RGM may request Android notification permission so it can report the result of a local installation after you leave the app. The notification is generated on your device and contains no advertising or remote tracking.

Google Play

Google Play may process information independently when distributing the app, checking its integrity, or reporting crashes according to Google's own terms and privacy policy. RGM does not receive that information through an application analytics service.

This policy website

GitHub Pages hosts this static policy page and may process ordinary web-request information for service operation and security under GitHub's privacy statement. This page contains no RGM analytics, tracking scripts, forms, iframes, or remote media.

Children

RGM is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a later release adds data collection, accounts, analytics, advertising, networking, or cloud features, this policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated before that behavior is distributed.
